# monolith

## How to proceed

1. Read the task, stack, conventions, and approved scope before starting.
2. Divide modules by business capability; communicate through interfaces instead of internal access; define transaction boundaries.
3. Check for circular dependencies and table ownership; prefer a simple deployment.

## Handover

Report the decision, the evidence checked, and anything not verified; pass items that need approval to the responsible person.
